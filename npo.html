FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden monthly hosting fees?

No. Your website is deployed on high-performance cloud hosting with zero monthly fees. The R1,000 fee covers the complete setup.

How much does the domain cost after the first year?

Domain registration is covered for 12 months. After 1 year, standard annual domain registry renewal is approximately R150/year for .org.za or .co.za.

How does Zoho Mail work?

Zoho provides a forever-free tier for small organizations with up to 5 user inboxes (5GB storage each). You receive custom emails on your domain without paying monthly software subscriptions. We configure the mail app on your phones and computers.

Can our website accept direct donor payments?

Yes. Your site includes an official Banking Details card with a 1-click "Copy Account" button so donors can do direct EFTs. If you want an online card payment gateway (like PayFast or Ozow), we can connect it upon request.

Who qualifies for this program?

Any registered South African Non-Profit Organisation (DSD NPO), Non-Profit Company (CIPC NPC), Community-Based Organisation (CBO), or registered Church/PBO holding valid registration credentials.
